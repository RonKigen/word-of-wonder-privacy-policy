Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Welcome to Words of Wonder. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed while you enjoy our word puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you download, access, or play our application.

1. Data Minimization & Local State

Words of Wonder is engineered with an offline-first architecture. Core game operations, including level state, puzzle progression, unlocked themes, star achievements, and coin balances, are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or maintain centralized databases containing your personal identity.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on your device configurations and active network services, the app may process limited technical data:

  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Non-personally identifiable technical telemetry (e.g., device model, operating system version, app crash logs, and screen resolution) to ensure smooth performance across various screen densities and hardware configurations.
  • Local Gameplay Analytics: Aggregated, local metrics regarding level completion times, hint usage rates, and level difficulty to continuously optimize game balance.

3. Third-Party Services & Native Integrations

If you opt to use connected features (such as cloud synchronization, developer analytics, or rewarded ad networks), standard third-party SDKs may automatically collect limited identifier data:

  • Platform Analytics & Crash Reporting: Tools such as Firebase Crashlytics or Google Play Services process crash traces to fix stability issues.
  • Advertising (If Enabled): Mobile ad network partners (such as AdMob) may collect anonymous advertising identifiers (IDFA or AAID) to serve relevant non-intrusive advertisements or reward coins upon opt-in video completion.

4. Children’s Privacy

Words of Wonder is designed for family audiences and word puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain legal jurisdictions). If you believe personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately to have the data removed.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

Because gameplay progress is saved locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data:

  • Clearing Data: You can wipe all app progress, saved state, and local settings at any time by clearing the application data in your device's settings menu or by uninstalling the app.
  • Opt-Out Options: You can reset or limit your device’s advertising identifier through your mobile operating system settings (Android / iOS).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or changes in regulatory requirements. Revised versions will be published on this page with an updated date.
